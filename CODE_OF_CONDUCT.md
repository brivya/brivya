# Community Code of Conduct

Brivya aims to maintain a professional, constructive and technically rigorous community.

Participants are expected to:

- discuss ideas and code, not attack people;
- give specific, actionable feedback;
- respect different backgrounds and experience levels;
- avoid harassment, discrimination and intimidation;
- protect confidential and security-sensitive information;
- assume good intent while remaining evidence-driven.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove content that violates these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

For conduct concerns, contact the maintainers privately rather than escalating sensitive matters publicly.
